# Privacy Policy — MadDragon

**Last updated:** 2026-09-05

---

## Overview

MadDragon is a singleplayer medieval strategy game built with Unity and distributed on Steam. This policy explains what data the game does and does not collect.

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

MadDragon does **not** collect, transmit, or store any of the following on external servers:

- Personal identification information (name, email address, date of birth)
- IP addresses or device identifiers
- Location data
- Payment or financial information
- Gameplay telemetry or analytics sent to our servers
- Chat or communication data
- User-generated content uploaded to external servers

---

## Local Data Storage

The game saves the following data **locally on your device only**:

| Data | Purpose | Location |
|------|---------|----------|
| Game settings (resolution, audio, display preferences) | Restore preferences on next launch | Local application data folder |
| Base configuration (unit placements, building layouts) | Save your current defensive setup | Local application data folder |
| Campaign progress and unlocks | Track Barracks level, unlocked units, wave history | Local application data folder |
| Resource state (gold, stored resources, protection rates) | Maintain economy state between sessions | Local application data folder |

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by removing the game's local save folder or by using the in-game "Reset Save Data" option.

---

## Future Online Features

A future update is planned to add **asynchronous PvP** — the ability to attack another player's pre-built base while that player is offline, and to have your base attacked in turn. When this feature is added:

- This privacy policy will be updated before the feature is released.
- An account or authentication system will be introduced, and the data collected by that system will be described in the updated policy.
- All online features will be opt-in.

---

## Steam Platform Data

MadDragon is distributed through Steam (Valve Corporation). Steam may collect platform-level data such as playtime, achievements, and crash reports according to the [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/). We have no control over and no access to data collected by Steam.

---

## Third-Party Services

MadDragon does not currently integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social SDKs. Unity Analytics and similar services are not active in the current build.

---

## Children's Privacy

MadDragon does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13. The game's current build has no online features, no account creation, and no communication channels.

---

## Changes to This Policy

When the game is updated to include online or account-based features, this policy will be updated and the new version will be posted on the Steam store page and within the game before those features become active.

---

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the Steam Community hub for MadDragon.
